# CH6 faster supported return v12

Select **CH6 HIGH before pulsing CH11** for the faster laydown; LOW or center
keeps the established normal return. The choice is latched independently of
stand-up. The successful fast stand-up remains v2, unchanged.

After the established forward fall and confirmed two-arm catch, fast return
can use three times the previous arm target speed: 1.80 rad/s with a 2.25-rad/s
motor cap. It accelerates over 600 ms, eases as the body falls faster, pauses
on lost arm load, and tapers near the floor. Existing catch and final flat/Forward
checks remain. [Policy and evidence](../evidence/balance-lower/fast-return-v12/README.md).

The latest physical v10 run took 5.910 s from support to first flat hold. The
model shows about twice the supported descent speed with v12, not three times
the whole maneuver. Physical speed and repeatability require the next trial.

Check [installed identity](progress/CURRENT.md) before testing. Stand as usual,
center CH1/CH2, select CH6 HIGH and pulse CH11 once. Observe continuous arm return
and the flat/Forward finish without CH12 assistance. Disarm both groups and
leave power on for the log download before another trial. Follow the existing
[test area and recovery procedure](BALANCE_TESTING.md).
